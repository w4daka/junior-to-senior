Strategic checklist

Print this. Ask before delegating to AI.

  • Interface first: can I describe inputs/outputs in one sentence?
  • General, not special: does one function replace N special cases?
  • Hidden complexity: what does the caller not need to know?
  • Out of scope: what will we explicitly not build?
  • Verify: what failing test proves it works?
