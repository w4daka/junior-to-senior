Spec your first program

Turn "I want a game" into 5 lines AI can build. 15 min.

Knowledge (3 min)

You already beat tactics: first.js runs. Strategy = decide what before AI writes how. A mini-spec has 5 lines: goal, input, output, one rule, not-doing.

Retrieval check (2 min)

Q1. Which line makes a spec buildable?

Skill task (8 min)

  1. Pick one: calculator, todo list, guess game.
  2. Copy the 5-line template into spec.txt.
  3. Fill it. Paste it here — that spec drives Lesson 0003 where AI builds it and you verify.
Win: one spec.txt an agent can implement with zero questions.
