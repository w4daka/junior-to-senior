Tactical vs strategic programming

One distinction that decides whether AI is your intern or your replacement. 15 min.

Knowledge (3 min)

Tactical programming = get it working now, move on. Strategic programming = invest a little extra to keep complexity down for every future change — better interfaces, hidden details, written-down why.

Retrieval check (2 min)

Q1. Which task is strategic?

Skill task (8 min, in your repo)

  1. Open your current JS/TS ticket. List 3 sub-tasks.
  2. Label each T (AI can do) or S (you must decide: interface, edge cases, what not to build).
  3. Rewrite one S item as a 2-sentence decision: "We will ___ so that ___. We won't ___."
Win: you have one strategic sentence AI cannot guess.
